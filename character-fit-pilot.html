Painted clothes.
Actually interchangeable.

Each garment is painted directly on the registered body, then extracted without scaling or warping. The result keeps the illustration quality of a finished character while allowing tops, bottoms, shoes, and aprons to be equipped independently.

Fit contract

Head and skin tone are independent selections. The selected palette is baked deterministically into the face, neck, arms, hands, and ankles. Garment pixels never change; jackets sit over tops and under aprons.

Identity scaling gate

Twelve forward-facing heads, four skin palettes

Every example uses the same straight-on pose, body silhouette, shirt, trousers, and shoes. Hair and facial details remain pixel-stable while the registered skin regions change palette.
