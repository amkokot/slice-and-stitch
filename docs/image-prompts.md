# Slice & Stitch — concept art prompts

Generation mode: built-in image generation tool.

## Town hub and home

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: browser game environment concept sheet
Primary request: create an original classic early-2000s browser game visual concept sheet for a cozy neighborhood hub in a cooking-and-fashion management game, working title Slice & Stitch. Do not imitate any specific existing game's characters or exact interface.
Scene/backdrop: a charming compact town block with four connected destinations: the player's small upstairs apartment/home, a cheerful corner pizzeria below it, a fabric-and-tools tailor shop, and a stylish clothing boutique. Show one larger hero view plus two or three small callout vignettes of the apartment dressing room and storefront details.
Subject: readable storefront silhouettes, warm pizzeria windows, awnings, clothing displays, sewing supplies, planter boxes, a bicycle, and a simple walkable street map feeling.
Style/medium: polished 2D digital concept art with thick ink outlines, soft cel shading, slightly exaggerated proportions, tactile painted texture, nostalgic Flash-era browser-game charm, but wholly original.
Composition/framing: landscape concept sheet, three-quarter cutaway/isometric-ish town view, clean margins, clear hierarchy, practical for game art direction.
Lighting/mood: late afternoon golden light, welcoming, playful, aspirational.
Color palette: tomato red, mozzarella cream, basil green, denim blue, warm brick, lavender accents.
Materials/textures: hand-painted brick, striped awnings, wood signs, fabric bolts, glossy window highlights.
Constraints: no logos, no copyrighted characters, no recognizable existing game UI, no watermark, no tiny explanatory text, no photorealism. Keep architecture implementable as layered 2D game backgrounds.
```

## Tailoring minigames

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: browser game tailoring minigame concept sheet
Primary request: create an original 2D fashion-crafting minigame concept sheet for a cooking-and-fashion management game, showing semi-realistic sewing actions translated into readable mouse/touch gameplay. Do not imitate any specific existing game's interface.
Scene/backdrop: cozy tailor workshop with wooden worktable, fabric shelves, dress form, sewing machine, cutting mat, iron, buttons and thread.
Subject: a central garment-in-progress and six clear visual station vignettes: 1) choose fabric and pattern, 2) pin a paper pattern, 3) trace and cut along a curved guide, 4) guide fabric under a sewing-machine needle while controlling speed, 5) press seams with an iron timing meter, 6) add buttons, trim, embroidery or patches. Include a final reveal on a dress form with quality tags represented by icons rather than text: material quality, precision, creativity, and a small sparkle/dice luck factor.
Style/medium: polished hand-drawn 2D browser game art, thick expressive outlines, soft cel shading, tactile fabrics, slightly exaggerated but mechanically believable tools, nostalgic classic Flash-era charm, fully original.
Composition/framing: landscape instructional concept sheet, large central workshop view with six evenly spaced close-up panels around it; strong hierarchy; big touch-friendly interaction zones; minimal text.
Lighting/mood: bright morning craft-room light, focused, satisfying, playful.
Color palette: denim blue, plum, butter yellow, mint, coral, warm wood, cream paper patterns.
Materials/textures: visible fabric weave, chalk marks, metal scissors, glossy buttons, thread spools, steam.
Constraints: no logos, no copyrighted characters, no recognizable existing game UI, no watermark, no dense tiny text, no photorealism. Hands and tools should be anatomically and mechanically plausible. Make the interaction gestures visually obvious using clean arrows, dotted guides, and meters.
```

## Unlockable food stations

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: browser game cooking minigame expansion concept sheet
Primary request: create an original 2D concept sheet for unlockable cooking stations in a cozy pizzeria-and-fashion management game: donut maker, gelato counter, and fresh pasta station. Translate plausible cooking actions into satisfying mouse/touch interactions with classic browser-game clarity. Do not imitate any specific existing game's interface.
Scene/backdrop: three coordinated countertop workstations inside the same warm pizzeria kitchen.
Subject: three equal columns. Donuts: mix batter, pipe rings into fryer, flip at the right moment, glaze and add toppings. Gelato: drag scoop through tubs with a curved motion, stack scoops with balance, add sauce and garnish. Pasta: combine flour and eggs, knead with rhythm, roll dough through machine, cut shape, boil to an al-dente timing zone, finish with sauce. Show small order cards and simple timing/accuracy meters made from icons and colored zones.
Style/medium: polished hand-drawn 2D browser game concept art, thick expressive outlines, soft cel shading, juicy food rendering, slightly exaggerated proportions, nostalgic Flash-era charm, fully original.
Composition/framing: landscape triptych concept sheet, large touch-friendly close-ups, clear sequential actions, minimal text, practical interface affordances.
Lighting/mood: cheerful, busy, rewarding; warm task lighting with glossy food highlights.
Color palette: cocoa brown and strawberry pink for donuts, pistachio green and berry purple for gelato, golden pasta and tomato red; shared cream and muted teal UI frames.
Materials/textures: glossy glaze, bubbling oil, creamy gelato, powdery flour, elastic dough, shiny sauce.
Constraints: no logos, no copyrighted characters, no recognizable existing game UI, no watermark, no tiny dense text, no photorealism. Food processes should be visually plausible and implementable as short skill minigames.
```

## Playable production plates — hybrid art pass

These assets are production background plates rather than full-screen concept art. The center of each plate is intentionally quiet so deterministic Canvas interactions can remain crisp and dependable above the painted art.

### Pizza preparation workstation

Saved as `assets/game/pizza-workstation-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production background plate for an interactive browser-game pizza minigame
Input images: Image 1 is the approved style reference. Match its warm hand-painted Flash-era aesthetic, inked contours, cozy pizzeria materials, color language, and level of finish; do not copy its multi-panel layout.
Primary request: a square, top-down pizza preparation workstation that can sit behind deterministic interactive pizza ingredients.
Scene/backdrop: flour-dusted honey-colored wooden prep counter inside the same neighborhood pizzeria; the edge of a green-tiled ingredient rail across the upper background; small bowls of tomato sauce, grated cheese, pepperoni, mushroom, and basil arranged around the outer perimeter; folded gingham towel, brass service bell, order ticket clipped at one corner; warm oven glow falling across the surface.
Subject: a large completely empty circular marble-and-wood pizza work board centered precisely in the image, occupying about 68% of the canvas. The center must remain unobstructed and low-detail so a programmatic pizza can be drawn over it.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted 2D game background, thick warm-brown outlines on props, soft cel shading plus restrained painterly texture, charming early-2000s browser-game readability, exactly consistent with Image 1.
Composition/framing: strict top-down square composition, symmetrical enough for gameplay, decorative props only in the outer 18% border, clear center, no perspective tilt.
Lighting/mood: warm welcoming pizzeria task light; neutral enough to accept morning/noon/dusk color overlays.
Color palette: tomato red, mozzarella cream, basil green, warm wood, muted teal, brass gold.
Constraints: no pizza, no dough, no hands, no characters, no text, no labels, no UI, no logos, no watermark. Do not place any prop over the central work board. Keep the central circle clearly bounded and uncluttered.
```
